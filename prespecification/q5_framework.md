# Q5: metabolic age normalisation thresholds

Reframed 2026-10-15. Q5 asks what thresholds would define metabolic age normalisation sufficient to justify structured treatment discontinuation.

## Why this section changed

The original framing implied empirical thresholds. There are none, and none can be derived from this review or from the literature it covers. Four things are missing simultaneously:

| Requirement | Status |
|---|---|
| Reference distributions for these biomarkers by age in a comparable population | Not assembled; would require cohort data outside this review |
| Evidence that reaching a threshold predicts outcome | Only hsCRP has any mediation evidence, and it marks ~32 per cent of the effect |
| Off-treatment biomarker trajectories after discontinuation | **No data exist for any glucagon receptor containing agent** |
| A trial randomising discontinuation on a biomarker threshold | Has never been conducted |

Proposing numeric thresholds against that evidence base would be the least defensible part of the paper, and the part most likely to draw a correction. It is therefore presented as a **framework with explicit assumptions**, each of which is testable and none of which is asserted as established.

## The precedent this borrows from, and where the analogy breaks

Type 2 diabetes remission has an agreed definition: HbA1c below 48 mmol/mol sustained at least three months after stopping glucose-lowering therapy (Riddle 2021 consensus). That definition works because three conditions hold which do not hold here.

| Condition for the diabetes remission definition | Holds for aging biomarkers? |
|---|---|
| A single marker captures the construct | No. The construct is multi-domain by definition |
| The marker has an agreed diagnostic threshold | No. None of our 20 markers has one for aging |
| Relapse is observable and clinically meaningful | Partially. Biomarker rebound is observable; its meaning is unknown |

The analogy is therefore structural, not substantive. It shows what a normalisation definition would need to look like, not what the thresholds should be.

## The framework

Four conditions, stated as what a future definition would have to satisfy.

### C1. Domain coverage, not composite score

A single composite crossing a threshold is insufficient, because a composite can be dragged across by one strongly-moving marker. Our own composite demonstrates this: under inverse-variance weighting hsCRP carried 85 per cent of it.

A normalisation definition should require that **each represented hallmark domain** independently reach its own criterion, not that an aggregate does.

### C2. Thresholds anchored to age-matched reference distributions, not to baseline change

Percentage change from baseline cannot define normalisation. A 40 per cent reduction from a very high starting value may not reach a normal range; a 10 per cent reduction from near-normal may already be within it.

Any threshold must be expressed as attainment of a value, referenced to an age-matched distribution. Those distributions are not assembled here and are the first concrete piece of work this framework requires.

### C3. Markers must be validated before they can carry a threshold

A threshold on an unvalidated marker is arithmetic. The surrogacy appraisal in this review grades every biomarker on outcome-prediction evidence. Only markers reaching B0 to B2 should be eligible to carry a discontinuation threshold.

On current evidence that is a very short list: **hsCRP, IL-6, NT-proBNP**, and of those the drug meaningfully moves only hsCRP.

### C4. Sustained off treatment, with a defined observation window

Normalisation sustained only during treatment is a treatment effect, not normalisation. The diabetes remission definition requires three months off therapy for this reason.

The evidence here is actively discouraging. Off-treatment weight regain is well documented (STEP-1 extension, SURMOUNT-4). No off-treatment aging biomarker data exist for any glucagon receptor containing agent. Until such data exist, no observation window can be specified, and C4 cannot be operationalised at all.

## What the review can state

That the four conditions above define what a metabolic age normalisation threshold would require, that none of the four is currently satisfiable, and that the specific missing pieces are identifiable and tractable:

| Missing piece | Who would supply it |
|---|---|
| Age-referenced distributions for validated aging markers | Cohort consortia; feasible now |
| Off-treatment biomarker trajectories | A discontinuation substudy in any ongoing phase 3 programme; cheap to add |
| Mediation analyses for markers beyond hsCRP | Secondary analyses of completed CVOTs with stored samples |

That is the realistic contribution: specifying the evidence a discontinuation decision would need, so that trials now being designed can generate it.

## What the review must not state

Numeric thresholds. Any specific figure offered now would be an expert opinion wearing the formatting of a result, and would be cited as a threshold long after the caveats were forgotten.

The section is explicitly labelled hypothesis-generating.
