# Screening pool from Alrasheed 2026

Built 2026-10-15 from the included-studies table of Alrasheed et al., Frontiers in Endocrinology, PMID 41625236, PMC12852008, PROSPERO CRD420251157476.

## The finding that matters most

Alrasheed's 40 trials cover **mono-agonists only**. Their own words: liraglutide 23 studies, exenatide 13, dulaglutide 2, and **no qualifying semaglutide RCTs**. They state explicitly:

> tirzepatide (a dual GIP/GLP-1 agonist) and triple agonists, which are more recent medications, were not included in our analysis

And in their future directions they call for precisely this review:

> head-to-head trials of various GLP-1 RA treatments (including more recent dual and triple agonists) with prospectively specified inflammatory and oxidative stress biomarker endpoints ... These studies would highlight whether variations in ... extra incretin effects (GIP co-agonism) result in significant variations in anti-inflammatory efficacy

That is Q2, named as the open question by the most recent meta-analysis in the field.

The same applies to Ren 2025, which pooled GLP-1 RAs as a single class without receptor stratification.

**The duplication concern is resolved.** Neither existing meta-analysis touches GIP dual, GCGR dual or triple agonism. Our corpus has all three. What they supply is the mono-agonist comparator depth we lack, which is the missing half of the contrast.

## Cautions carried from Alrasheed

Their risk of bias assessment found **39 of 40 trials at "some concerns"**, with only one at low risk. Roughly 35 per cent raised concerns about selective outcome reporting. Any pooled estimate built on this trial set inherits that.

Their sensitivity analyses repeatedly show that removing one or two outliers eliminates heterogeneity and, in several cases, eliminates the effect. TNF-alpha against other oral agents fell from significant to null (SMD −0.23, 95% CI −0.66 to 0.19) once two outliers were removed. IL-6 monotherapy likewise became null. These are not robust findings and should not be presented as such.

Nineteen of 40 trials were conducted in China, which they flag as a generalisability limit.

## Note on our own search

These 40 trials were found by a search our review has not executed. Including them means adopting another group's search, which must be stated in the Methods: our corpus for the mono-agonist comparator derives from the included-study lists of two published systematic reviews, supplemented by our own anchor searching for dual and triple agonists, which those reviews did not cover.

That is defensible and is how overviews of reviews work, but it is not the same as an independent search and will be described accurately.
