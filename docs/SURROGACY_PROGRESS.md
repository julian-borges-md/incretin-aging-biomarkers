## Early observations, provisional

**hsCRP is the only B1 in the inventory so far and may be the only one.** It is the sole biomarker for which a mediation analysis exists within the same trial population that the drug was tested in. If the remaining markers land at B3 or below, the honest reading is that the aging biomarker signature is largely responsive but unvalidated, with one validated exception.

That would be a more useful finding than a composite implying the whole signature carries clinical meaning, and it identifies exactly where validation effort should go.

**The population mismatch problem is likely to recur.** GDF-15's prediction evidence comes from atrial fibrillation and cancer cohorts, not from obesity. The same will probably be true of several markers whose epidemiology was built in elderly or disease-specific populations while the drug trials run in middle-aged people with obesity. Grading this strictly is what keeps the appraisal meaningful; grading it loosely would promote most of the inventory to B2 on populations that do not match.

**GDF-15 carries a direction-of-meaning problem worth stating in the manuscript.** It rises with metformin and with cancer cachexia. A fall in GDF-15 is not unambiguously favourable in a weight-loss context, and treating it as an anti-aging signal because it predicts mortality in other settings would be the exact error this component exists to prevent.

## Next

Grade leptin and adiponectin, where cohort evidence is likely plentiful and the population match to obesity is closer than for GDF-15. Then lean mass, which is the only unfavourable axis and the one with the clearest functional outcome literature.
