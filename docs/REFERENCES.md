# Anchor Reference Corpus

Grouped by the role each plays in the argument.

## Epigenetic aging, the evidence that makes the question answerable

| # | Reference | DOI |

| 3 | Guarente L, Sinclair DA, Kroemer G. Human trials exploring anti-aging medicines. Cell Metab. 2024;36(2):354-376 | 10.1016/j.cmet.2023.12.007 |

Reference 1 is the single most important paper in the corpus. It is the first randomized evidence that any incretin based therapy modulates validated epigenetic aging clocks, and no equivalent data exist for any dual or triple agonist.

## The GDF-15 receptor class differential

| # | Reference | DOI |

Tirzepatide reduced GDF-15; liraglutide did not change it. No GDF-15 data exist for any GCGR containing agent, which makes retatrutide the single most informative unmeasured molecule in the class.

## Weight independent metabolic effects of GCGR agonism

| # | Reference | DOI |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Ekinci EI, et al. Survodutide improves biomarkers of beta-cell function and insulin sensitivity. Diabetes Obes Metab. 2026;28(9):8242-8253 | 10.1111/dom.70861 |
| 11 | Banerjee R, et al. Weight as determinant of MASLD treatment response. Diabetes Obes Metab. 2025;28(1):44-52 | 10.1111/dom.70187 |

Reference 10 reports improvements in HOMA-IR and adiponectin described as largely independent of bodyweight change. Reference 11 finds weight loss explains 54% of fibrosis improvement, leaving 46% unattributed to any receptor mechanism. That residual is the quantity this project's hepatic meta-regression is built to interrogate.

## Hepatic outcomes by receptor class

| # | Reference | DOI |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | Sanyal AJ, et al. Semaglutide for MASH (ESSENCE). N Engl J Med. 2025;392(22):2226-2238 | 10.1056/NEJMoa2413258 |
| 13 | Loomba R, et al. Tirzepatide for MASH (SYNERGY-NASH). N Engl J Med. 2024;391(4):299-310 | 10.1056/NEJMoa2401943 |
| 14 | Sanyal AJ, et al. Survodutide for MASH. N Engl J Med. 2024;391(4):311-319 | 10.1056/NEJMoa2401755 |
| 15 | Sanyal AJ, Kaplan LM, Frias JP, et al. Triple hormone receptor agonist retatrutide for MASLD: a randomized phase 2a trial. Nat Med. 2024;30(7):2037-2048 | 10.1038/s41591-024-03018-2 |

Reference 15 is the retatrutide hepatic anchor: liver fat fell 81.4% at 8 mg and 82.4% at 12 mg, with 86% reaching a normal liver fat fraction at 12 mg against 0% on placebo. The near identical effect at 8 and 12 mg, while weight loss continues to increase across that interval, is the dose response feature that argues against pure weight mediation. Retatrutide has no biopsy confirmed MASH trial, so its hepatic effect is established at imaging but not histology.

## Body composition and the unfavorable aging axis

| # | Reference | DOI |
|---|---|---|

| 18 | Nong K, et al. Comparative effects of drugs for adults with overweight or obesity: network meta-analysis. BMJ. 2026;394:e372161 | 10.1136/bmj-2026-372161 |

Reference 16 frames lean mass loss as comparable to a decade or more of aging. ## Cardiovascular, framing and precedent

| # | Reference | DOI |
|---|---|---|
| 26 | Nicholls SJ, et al. SURPASS-CVOT tirzepatide vs dulaglutide. N Engl J Med. 2025;393(24):2409-2420 | 10.1056/NEJMoa2505928 |
| 27 | Savas M, et al. Beyond weight loss: multisystem benefits of obesity medications. Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol. 2026;14(8):678-692 | 10.1016/S2213-8587(26)00100-2 |
| 28 | Paschou SA, et al. GLP-1RA and skin aging. Endocrine. 2025 | 10.1007/s12020-025-04293-w |
| 29 | Riddle MC, et al. Consensus report: definition and interpretation of remission in type 2 diabetes. Diabetes Care. 2021;44(10):2438-2444 | 10.2337/dci21-0034 |

Reference 29 is the methodological precedent for the normalization thresholds. It defined remission before prospective trials had tested whether patients meeting the definition experienced different long term outcomes, and the definition was valuable because it converted an unstructured clinical question into a measurable one. The thresholds proposed here are offered in the same spirit and carry the same provisional status.
